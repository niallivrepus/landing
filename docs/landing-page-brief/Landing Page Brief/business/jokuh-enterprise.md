## Section 3 — Private TEE deployment

**Body (90 words):**
For Enterprise customers, Jokuh's compute is deployed inside a TEE configuration that the customer controls. The TEE produces attestations the customer's auditors can verify directly — not via Jokuh's word.

This is the difference that matters in regulated procurement. The customer no longer has to take confidentiality on faith. The cryptographic facts of the deployment are visible, signed, and continuously verifiable.

---

## Section 4 — Identity and access

**Body (70 words):**
Enterprise integrates with your identity provider via SAML or OIDC, with SCIM provisioning for joiners and leavers. Sigil bindings can be linked to corporate identity for assured attribution. When an employee leaves, their Sigil's organizational scope is revoked at the cryptographic layer — keys for shared scopes are rotated automatically.
