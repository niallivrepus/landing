## Section 2 — The problem you actually have

**Headline:**
Your team is using AI. Your compliance officer doesn't know where the data went.

**Body (100 words):**
Most teams have already adopted AI through the back door — pasted into ChatGPT, sent to Copilot, dropped into a personal Claude window. Compliance is racing to catch up. The tools that try to catch the data after the fact treat the symptom, not the cause.

Jokuh treats the cause. The tools your team uses every day — chat, voice, transcription, search, agentic action — run on a stack where data confidentiality is enforced cryptographically, before the data ever leaves your control.
