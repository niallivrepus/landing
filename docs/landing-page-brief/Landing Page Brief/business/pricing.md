## Section 2 — The pricing principle

**Body (90 words):**
Most software charges you per user and incentivizes spreading thin: more seats, less depth. Jokuh prices the opposite shape — we want each user to use more of the system, more deeply, over time.

That is why a typical Jokuh customer increases revenue per user as they adopt more of the surface (Spine storage, Calls, Blurbs, marketplace, Wallet activity) — not by adding seats. The math, for our buyers, is the math of an operating system, not a subscription app.

## Section 7 — Frequently asked

**Q: Do you charge per seat?**
For Jokuh Business and Enterprise, seats matter, but depth matters more. Talk to us for a real quote.

**Q: Can I try before I buy?**
Yes. The consumer tiers are available through TestFlight today. Business pilots are available for qualified teams.

**Q: Do prices change later?**
Existing tiers are honored for existing users. We do not retroactively reprice.

**Q: Are there volume discounts?**
For Enterprise, yes. For consumer, no — we believe in flat, public pricing for individuals.
