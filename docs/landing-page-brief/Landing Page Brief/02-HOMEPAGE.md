## Section 2 — The split

**Headline:**
Today's AI runs on you. Jokuh runs for you.

**Body (max 90 words):**
Every prompt you type into a mainstream AI is logged, retained, and — by court order or business model — eventually used. Privacy by policy is rewritten at 2 AM. Privacy by mathematics is not.

Jokuh is built so the company that builds it cannot read your data. Encryption keys stay with you. Computation happens inside a Trusted Execution Environment. Verification is mathematical, not promised.

**Inline pattern:** Two-column comparison, simple, no glow effects.

| Most AI | Jokuh |
|---|---|
| Cloud-hosted, account-bound | Local-first, peer-to-peer |
| One model. One vendor. | Model-agnostic |
| Your data trains them | Your data stays yours |
| Privacy by policy | Confidentiality by cryptography |
| Stateless chat | Persistent companion |

## Section 4 — The how (architecture, public layer)

**Headline:**
Confidentiality, by construction.

**Three pillars (icon + 1 line each):**
- **TEE** — Trusted Execution Environment. Code runs in a vault. Even Jokuh cannot peek.
- **ZKP** — Zero-Knowledge Proofs. Prove a computation happened, without revealing the data.
- **FHE** — Fully Homomorphic Encryption. Compute on data while it stays encrypted.

**Footnote line:** Verify ↔ Trust. → `/products/spine#architecture`
