## Section 2 — The bio is dead

**Headline:**
A handle is not an identity.

**Body (90 words):**
For two decades the answer to "who are you online" has been a username, a 160-character bio, and a feed you do not own. Each platform takes a slice. None of them are portable. All of them can be revoked.

Profile is built on a different premise. Your identity is not a handle. It is the trail of the way you think — the people you talk to, the ideas you return to, the questions you keep asking. The Sigil expresses that.

---

## Section 3 — What is a Sigil? (anchor: `#sigil`)

**Body (110 words):**
A Sigil is a living digital identity. It is a cryptographic object that represents you, owned by your keys, evolving with what your Spine learns and what you choose to publish. Think of it as the union of:

- A self-sovereign DID (decentralized identifier)
- A Knowledge Pool of your real thinking, summarized to a public surface that you control
- A reputational layer earned through consistency, not influence
- A visual identity expressed through a generative glyph that responds to your activity

The Sigil is not given to you by Jokuh. Jokuh is the place where you can finally hold one.

## Section 5 — Private mode

**Body (70 words):**
Every Sigil has a public face and a private interior. The interior is your full Knowledge Pool — never shown, never indexable, used only to inform agents you have authorized.

You can present a different facet of your Sigil to different scopes: a professional facet for collaborators, a personal facet for friends, an anonymous facet for the parts of life that need to stay anonymous.

Identity by context, not by platform.

## Section 6 — What this enables

- **One identity across surfaces.** Your Sigil works inside Jokuh, on the open web, and across the wallets and chains you already use.
