## Section 2 — The shift

**Headline:**
You already say smart things every day. The internet just doesn't see them.

**Body (90 words):**
Every meeting. Every voice memo. Every Slack thread. Insight is generated constantly and lost constantly. The current attention economy demands that you stop thinking long enough to stage a post about thinking. So you don't post. So your ideas die in transcripts no one will read.

Blurbs invert that. Your AI listens to what you are already saying and generates a stream of low-cost, high-signal seeds. You glance, you publish, or you keep them to yourself.

---

## Section 3 — What is a Blurb?

**Headline:**
A blurb is not a tweet.

**Body (80 words):**
A tweet is a complete thought ready to publish. A blurb is the seed before the tweet — an idea in emergence. Inherently incomplete. Low computational cost. Carries the context of where it came from. Private by default. Public only by explicit flag.

Calling something *a blurb* means: this seed could become something. Let the AI explore it. You decide whether to plant it.

---

## Section 4 — The three layers it draws from

**Three columns:**

1. **Chrome plugin**
   Captures Google Meet, Zoom, Slack huddles, Discord calls, directly in browser. Full speaker attribution when present.

2. **Hardware (when available)**
   Ambient capture of nearby spoken audio. The Jokuh hardware records information you were exposed to — *indulged transcription* — and stores it in a separate Spine partition.

3. **Native Jokuh**
   Calls and Messages inside Jokuh itself feed Blurbs with complete metadata, real-time crystallization, and consent-aware speaker attribution.

---

## Section 5 — How distribution works (anchor: `#example`)

**Three states a blurb can be in:**

- **Private** — sits in your Spine, used to prime your agents and inform other blurbs. Default.
- **Scoped** — shared with named identities, a group, or a Pod. The blurb carries access controls with it.
- **Public** — published to your profile feed. One-click. Reversible.

**Body line:** Posting becomes a routing decision, not a creation chore.

## Section 6 — Three example flows

**Card 1 — The strategist**
You finish a Zoom call about Q3 GTM. Your Spine crystallizes the call. Three blurbs surface: a punchline framing, a counter-take you almost said but cut, and a question worth asking publicly. You ship the question.

**Card 2 — The researcher**
A reading session with your hardware on. Twelve hours of papers. Two days later, four blurbs surface comparing arguments across them. You scope two to your collaborators and keep two private.

**Card 3 — The founder**
Investor meetings all week. Spine sees patterns across feedback. Eight blurbs surface — five private, three you ship as public learnings. The next investor reads them before the call.

## Section 8 — Privacy posture

**Body (60 words):**
Blurbs inherit Spine's confidentiality model. Source transcripts never leave the encrypted enclave. Generated blurbs are computed inside the TEE. Speaker attribution is consent-tracked. The hardware-captured *indulged* layer is treated separately from your spoken words and weighted differently in everything Jokuh does on your behalf.
