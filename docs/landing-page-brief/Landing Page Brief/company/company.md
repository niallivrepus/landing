## Section 5 — How we operate

**Body (90 words):**
Jokuh is a small, intentionally focused team. The company is structured so user data is never inside the operating company's reach. Investments and corporate structure are designed with that constraint as a starting point, not an afterthought.

We work in public when the work allows it, and in private when the cryptography demands it. We pay our writers and our hardware collaborators. We do not sell user data. We do not host an admin layer that reads user content.
