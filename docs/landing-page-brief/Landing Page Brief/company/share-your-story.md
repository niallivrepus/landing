# Share your story — `/company/share-your-story`

**Purpose:** A clean, low-friction submission surface for two distinct audiences: (1) people willing to share how they use Jokuh, (2) writers pitching essays for the Stories editorial program. Make it obvious which is which.

**Audience:** Users, writers, journalists, partners.

---

## Section 3 — Form A: User story submission

**Fields:**
- Name (optional)
- Sigil handle (optional, alternative to name)
- Email (required, encrypted in transit)
- How are you using Jokuh? (textarea, no character cap)
- Anything we should not publish? (textarea)
- Attribution preference: anonymous · first name only · full name · my Sigil
- Consent checkbox: "I have authorized Jokuh to consider this story for publication. I understand publication is not guaranteed."

**Submit copy:** Send story →

**Privacy note (immediately under submit button):**
*Your submission is encrypted in transit and stored under the same posture we apply to user content: scope-aware, key-controlled, never sold.*

---

## Section 4 — Form B: Editorial pitch

**Fields:**
- Name (required)
- Email (required)
- Editorial pillar — dropdown:
  - Cryptographic conviction
  - Sovereign software
  - Lived sovereignty
  - Other
- Working title (required)
- Pitch (textarea, 200-word target)
- Examples of prior work (URLs or upload, optional)
- Compensation expectation (optional, free text)

**Submit copy:** Send pitch →

**Editorial note (immediately under submit button):**
*We respond within 14 days. We accept fewer than we receive. We pay on acceptance, edit collaboratively, and do not impose ad-style edits.*

---

## Section 5 — What we don't publish

**Headline:** What we will say no to.

**Body (60 words):**
- Bookkeeping pieces about the AI hype cycle.
- Vendor comparisons.
- Pieces that could be a press release.
- Anything that asks the reader to trust without giving them a way to verify.

We are not against any of those. We just publish a different kind of work.
