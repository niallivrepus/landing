## Section 2 — What working here is like

**Body (110 words):**
We are a small distributed team. The work is dense. The expectations are real. We do not run interview gauntlets — we do paid trials with people we are seriously considering.

We pay competitively, equity is meaningful, and your name shows up next to your work. We respect deep-work time. We don't have status meetings. We do have hard reviews of our own architecture, in writing, every release.

If you have spent years wishing software cared more about its users than its investors, you will recognize this place quickly.

---

## Section 3 — How we hire

**Three steps, single line each:**

1. **Letter** — write to the lead for the role. Tell us what you've built and what you want to build next.
2. **Trial** — paid, scoped, real. Two weeks at most. Real codebase or real design system.
3. **Decision** — we are honest, fast, and we tell you why.

---

## Section 4 — What we look for

**Body (90 words):**
Range and depth. We hire people who have built systems and shipped them, and who can write clearly enough to be trusted with the architecture of something that has to outlast its founders.

We weight three signals especially heavily:
- *Cryptographic seriousness* — you understand why "trust the company" is not an answer.
- *Architectural clarity* — you can see five steps ahead, and explain why.
- *Honesty under pressure* — you tell the truth before the deadline, not after.
