## Section 2 — The why

**Headline:**
The window is closing.

**Body (110 words):**
Every day, billions of people speak their most private thoughts into AI systems they do not own and cannot audit. The conversations become training data, evidence, and inventory — in that order. This is not a conspiracy. This is the business model.

The pattern is the same one social media followed twenty years ago, except deeper. Social media captured what you shared. AI captures what you think. The extraction has moved from the surface to the source.

We are choosing to build the door before the door is no longer possible to build.

---

## Section 3 — The principle

**Headline:**
Do not trust. Verify.

**Body (90 words):**
Privacy is a policy. Confidentiality is a guarantee. The first can be rewritten in a 2 AM TOS update. The second cannot be rewritten without breaking math.

Jokuh runs on cryptographic primitives — Trusted Execution Environments, Zero-Knowledge Proofs, Fully Homomorphic Encryption — vetted by independent researchers and engineered into every layer of the operating system.

We are designed so the company that ships Jokuh is structurally incapable of reading user data. There is no admin override. There is no privileged backdoor. There is nothing to hand over.

## Section 4 — The mission
