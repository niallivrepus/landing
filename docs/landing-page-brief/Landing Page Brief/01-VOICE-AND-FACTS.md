## 1. Voice

**Frequency:** Mitchell × Martin merged. Manifesto-grade. Joyful. Sharp. Forward-leaning.

**Texture in one paragraph:**
Jokuh writes like someone who has read the whole stack and decided to be kind anyway. The voice carries the weight of cryptography and the lightness of a friend handing you the keys. Sentences are short when they need to land, and longer when they need to breathe. Never apologetic. Never theatrical. We are not selling — we are inviting.

**Permitted moves:**
- Open with a fact, never a hedge.
- Use the word *sovereignty* sparingly — earn it.
- One short manifesto-line per page is allowed. More than one becomes noise.
- Symbolic punctuation (→, ↔, ⇌, ⇾) is allowed in micro-copy and CTAs only — never in body paragraphs. Reserve it for hero buttons, footers, badges.
- Joy is a real signal. The word is welcome.

**Disallowed moves:**
- "Revolutionize", "disrupt", "game-changer", "next-generation". Burned tokens.
- Em-dash drama. One per paragraph max.
- Stacked superlatives. ("The most beautiful, most secure, most powerful...")
- Tech-bro authority voice. We are wizards, not warlords.
- AI hype language. Jokuh does not "leverage AI" — Jokuh runs on it.

**Reading level:** Smart 16-year-old. Curious adult. Crypto-literate but not insider-only.

**Sentence rhythm:** Short. Then medium. Then a longer one that lets the reader breathe before the next short hit.

## 8. Imagery guidance

- Dark base. Generous negative space.
- Type-forward. The brand reads like a literary publisher more than a SaaS.
- Iconography stays geometric and quiet — circles, sigils, gates, keys. No glossy 3D.
- Photography: people thinking, not people posing. Hardware shots matte, never glossy.
- Motion: slow. Confident. No bounce. Easings should feel like the door of a vault, not a snap.
