humanity is doing something it has never done before.

## 2

## 3

Into servers they cannot audit.

Under terms they have never read.

Controlled by people they did not elect.

## 5

- More lawyers draft strategy inside it.
- More families trust it with their most intimate moments.
- More governments integrate it into infrastructure.
- More classrooms that teach children depend on it to define what is factual.

The data doesn't disappear.

It accumulates. It compounds. It becomes leverage, And leverage, in the wrong hands, becomes control.

- Mass cognitive surveillance architecture already exists
- AI makes it exponentially more powerful + accessible
